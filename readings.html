Reading Reflection #1

The reading by Susan Sontag made my understanding of the use and effect of photography clearer. I think photography, as described in the passage, is a way to freeze a moment in one's life; however, there is a much more personal impact of photography that not many people really consider, including myself. Photography can capture some very vulnerable moments that capture the attention of many and leave the audience wanting more. The way in which I use photography is to capture small moments that capture my attention; usually, these would be when I’m outside taking a walk through my neighborhood and looking around to see people simply being people or just a landscape, which I like. I often make it a point to photograph these moments because they are precious to me, and in some ways, they even inspire me to create. In Sontag's book, where it describes the meaning of photography, I strongly agree that it’s a way to immortalize life and remember a moment with one simple picture. Whenever I take a picture of anything, I don’t necessarily think about it in that moment in time. Still, when I end up looking back at those pictures, I find myself recalling the moment. Even the day, and when I do so, I find myself getting nostalgic, remembering the feeling I was experiencing when taking those photos. When creating graphics, I want to be able to entertain people, much like what inspires me to capture photos in a certain way when designing anything; I want to keep my viewers entertained and be taken away by the colors, the placement of shapes and lines, and how the words (if any) are designed within the page. When thinking about the current state of the world, I would say photography can be used to describe current events for later generations in different ways; for example, photos about police brutality, the vast number of protests for different human rights, separation of family or maybe even a precious moment like a picture of community coming together to evoke emotion from those viewing it to see how a group of people where done wrong, these would be some of how I would use photography to represent the current events of the world. With illustrations, I would imagine I would take a similar approach; I would create a more selective way of interpreting the current moments in our world, something that may not be too obvious to everyone but is easy enough to decipher by those who feel represented by it, I would want to possibly even use some of the say tactics which I would use for photographing these moment’s although not in it entirety as it may take away from the intent of the illustration, I may even simply create something small combining the two only add small illustrations to the photograph to direct the viewer to a specific place in the image where the message I want to sent would be located. I think the information given in Sontag’s passage is quite informative and provided me with a deeper insight into how photography can be used to tell a story and ultimately even influence the perception of a moment.

Reading Reflection #2

After reading the MIT article and watching the video from The Diary of a CEO, I think there is a lot to consider about AI and its effects on the brain. I do think that AI can heavily impact our thinking; after all, we would rely on a machine to tell us information rather than retrieving it ourselves. I think it's important to note that AI can make up information, so it really shouldn't be used to get information unless the person using it can do their own research to verify what AI gives them. The article by MIT had an interesting experiment with the essays that were written using different techniques, and I would say the outcome of the essay wasn't that hard to believe. I think when people use AI, they aren't absorbing anything in terms of context. I think AI tries to shorten the information that it gives, and therefore it's not providing the user with more information on a subject. I think had the essay been fully peer-reviewed, it wouldn't be surprising to know that much of the information was taken out of context and serves no purpose in the essay. The video also provides some interesting questions about AI and its effects on children, and also about the way AI makes us think less. I think a good point in the video is made when they test out the information that ChatGPT needs in order to provide information. "Do we need to spell?" I think it's interesting to see how easily AI can decipher a message, and in some ways it can be useful for people who maybe don't know how to correctly spell a word and maybe only know the spelling slightly, and AI is maybe a faster way to get the word's meaning; however, I believe that this can be hurtful. People who use ChatGPT may end up giving up on spelling, knowing it will be fixed by AI, or even cause AI to confuse itself and lead to people being given incorrect information, which may not be looked over. Based on the video, AI has a lot of power over what information we get, but if not correctly looked over, it's a disservice to those using it, as not much thought is put into what we're asking AI. My own take from both is that AI can be good if used correctly, which it very often is not used correctly, but if used the right way, it can be helpful for certain circumstances; however, it should be used in moderation, and when used, should be followed by a person's own background research to further their understanding of a subject or may just verify the information, as well as create a way for them to actively use the information they received from AI to get their brains to be used more and retain the information which they received with AI however avoiding AI in general should be done as much as possible due to the impact it can have with the user.

Reading Reflection #3

After reading the story Kadiatu: The grieving mother “The Devil at my Door,” I believe the reason for the illustration was mainly to tell the story with emotion and to describe the thoughts that are being experienced during this moment. I would also imagine that the use of illustration was to fill in the blanks of information that aren’t fully exhibited by using photographs. I think the way in which the illustrations vary from photographs in terms of this story is the fact that it seems as though the illustrations add to the intensity of the story being told. An example of this can be seen in the illustration of the traditional healer, which is depicted as a simple dark figure; in my opinion, it helps to intensify the character, as although we are given dialogue about the healer, the way in which he is depicted as a dark figure helps in really solidifying the type of character he is. I would also say it creates tension, as certain moments would show Kadiatu surrounded by darkness. I would also say I feel like the way in which her environment is depicted in the illustrations is used to show the environment, but also it doesn’t overdo the depiction, possibly to keep the focus on the story and not bring too much attention to the surroundings. I think some of the advantages of illustrations over text only or photography are that, with only text, the story would be perceived differently; the reader would most likely envision the story differently, and therefore the impact of the story would change, or valuable information that could be provided by illustration would be lost. I would say, in comparison with photography, it changes the way in which the illustrations have an advantage; it creates contrast, which, much like text, strengthens the story being told and provides a more narrative structure. I would say it also impacts how we perceive the moment; one example would be at the end, where Kadiatu’s son Aboul got sick. The illustration really intensifies the reaction and also the state in which her son is in although we are not completely sure if this is how this moment happened we do know that this is most likely what the moment felt like which gives the story more power. I do think that the photographs which are included in the story do add to the story in their own ways. For instance, for this particular story, the images show the environment in which Kadiatu is in and make the moment seem more intimate. Some of the other photographs that are used show her family, which I feel is used not just to tie the story together but also to create a sense of relaxation in the story, as we see a now healthy, beautiful family spending time together; it's also very intimate, which is maybe something that the illustration could not have provided just the same. I would say the way in which each style is incorporated into the story really does help the story and its meaning in their own way and shape the way in which it's read.
